Privacy

SilentEarn publishes educational articles and resources about AI tools, digital skills, freelancing, product research, and realistic online opportunities. This page explains the basic data handling associated with this website.

Information you submit

If you submit a contact or partnership form, the website sends the information you provide—such as your name, email address, optional phone number, request type, reference, and message—to SilentEarn through its contact API. If email delivery is configured, the details are routed to the SilentEarn inbox so the enquiry can be answered. Do not submit sensitive personal or financial information through the form.

SilentEarn Notes requests

The SilentEarn Notes form currently sends a request to SilentEarn's contact endpoint. It does not automatically add you to a newsletter provider or deliver automated emails unless a separate email subscription system is configured. A success message means the request was received by the current form endpoint, not necessarily that a mailing-list subscription is active.

Saved items

The Save for Later feature uses browser local storage on the device where you save an item. Saved items are not currently synchronised between devices and are not attached to a SilentEarn user account.

External services and links

The website may link to external providers, social platforms, affiliate destinations, hosting, and email delivery services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. When you leave SilentEarn, review the destination's privacy terms.

Affiliate and sponsored content

Some product links may be affiliate links. Where relevant, SilentEarn may earn a commission when a visitor purchases or signs up through an eligible link. Sponsored relationships, where accepted, should be identified clearly in the relevant content.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email SilentEarnn@gmail.com.
