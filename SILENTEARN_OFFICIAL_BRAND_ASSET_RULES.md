# SilentEarn Official Creator and Logo Assets

## Official status
The creator photographs and SilentEarn logo supplied by the owner are the **actual official brand assets**, not generic references or sample placeholders.

## Asset inventory
- Primary creator portrait: use for the creator/profile, About section, author panel, and branded visuals when the creator is intended to appear.
- Official SilentEarn logo lockup: use exactly as supplied, preserving its emblem, lettering, colours, proportions, and transparency.
- Secondary creator photograph: real creator photo, but other people are visible in its background. Do not use it in public promotional artwork by default; prefer the primary portrait unless the owner specifies a particular use.

The downloadable brand asset pack contains the three unaltered original PNGs and separate web-optimized copies. The current pack uses these intended web paths when its web copies are uploaded:
- `assets/brand/silenteearn-official-logo.webp`
- `assets/brand/silenteearn-official-symbol.webp`
- `assets/creator/silenteearn-creator-portrait.webp`

## Mandatory rules
1. Treat the uploaded creator photographs and logo as SilentEarn's real official identity, not merely optional reference suggestions.
2. If a graphic or page shows the SilentEarn creator, use the supplied creator image and preserve the recognizable identity, skin tone, hairstyle, and natural facial features. Never substitute an AI-generated person.
3. Use the exact supplied logo. Never redesign, retype, recolour, stretch, skew, replace, or regenerate it. Use a symbol crop only for compact contexts such as a favicon; keep the original full logo as the source of truth.
4. Preserve the uploaded original files unchanged. Cropping, resizing, or compression must be a separate derivative.
5. Blog illustrations that do not depict the creator may use relevant conceptual artwork. Such artwork must not substitute for the official creator photo or logo.
6. Do not upload the secondary portrait to public promotional placements by default because other people appear in its background.
7. Maintain the surrounding design palette: Midnight Navy `#0B1220`, Electric Teal `#18C6B4`, Soft White `#F7F9FC`, Slate Gray `#64748B`. Do not recolour the actual logo to fit a design.
8. If the actual asset is not accessible in the current working context, ask the owner to attach it or clearly mark the relevant deliverable as pending. Never claim that an official image was used if it was not available.

## Website integration status
The official asset pack is prepared separately. Until the web copies have been added to the GitHub repository and the homepage markup points to them, do not claim the live site uses the official image/logo. The repository is public, so files added under web paths become publicly accessible.
