01 / Start with the problem

Before comparing features, define the job you need the product to do. A popular product can still be wrong for your use case.

What problem am I trying to solve?

Describe the task, frustration, or outcome you need.

How do I solve it today?

List your current tool, manual method, or workaround.

What would a useful result look like?

Define a realistic outcome you can observe or measure.

What is my budget and time limit?

Include subscriptions, add-ons, setup time, and learning time.

02 / Verify the product

Use the official website, pricing page, terms, documentation, privacy information, and credible independent sources. Mark unknown details as unknown.
